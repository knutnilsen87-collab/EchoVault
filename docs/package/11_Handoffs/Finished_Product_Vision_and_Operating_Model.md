# Finished Product Vision and Operating Model

## When this product is mature, what does it do?
EchoVault becomes the trusted licensing layer for high-quality AI/hybrid music. Buyers can find a track, understand its provenance, select a license, pay, receive proof/files and get support if claims arise. Artists can monetize high-effort AI/hybrid production through a curated channel instead of competing in undifferentiated AI music spam.

## Who uses it?
- Creators needing monetized content music.
- Agencies producing commercial campaigns.
- Indie game teams and motion designers.
- SMB marketing teams.
- AI/hybrid producers and artists.
- Internal reviewers/support/admins.

## How does it operate?
- Artists submit tracks and PoE.
- EchoScore screens technical quality.
- Human reviewers approve/reject.
- Approved tracks enter catalog.
- Buyers license tracks through self-serve checkout.
- License records are immutable and certificates are generated.
- High-res files are delivered through signed URLs.
- Disputes can suspend future sales while preserving historical records.
- Analytics and support signals inform catalog and review policy.

## What must be true for it to feel finished?
- Buyers trust the certificate and understand permitted use.
- Track quality is consistently high.
- Artist onboarding is clear.
- Review time is predictable.
- Support can handle claims/disputes.
- Legal terms are defensible.
- Payment/download flows are reliable.
- Catalog supply matches buyer demand.
- Product has evidence of repeat purchases.
