# Terms and Policies Notes

## Required user-facing policy documents
1. Terms of Service
2. Privacy Policy
3. Buyer License Agreement
4. Artist Submission Agreement
5. Artist Rights Warranty
6. Acceptable Use Policy
7. Refund / Digital Delivery Policy
8. Dispute / Takedown Policy
9. AI Provenance Disclosure
10. Cookie/analytics notice if applicable

## Policy decisions needed
- Are licenses perpetual?
- Are licenses worldwide?
- Are licenses transferable?
- Are refunds allowed after file access?
- What happens if a track is later disputed?
- What support does buyer receive for copyright/Content ID claim?
- What royalties/payout timing do artists receive?
- What evidence must artist provide for sample/license claims?
- What jurisdictions are supported at launch?

## Prohibited claims
Avoid:
- “risk-free”
- “copyright-proof”
- “safe harbor”
- “guaranteed claim-free”
- “fully cleared” unless legally accurate and reviewed

Preferred:
- “documented”
- “reviewed”
- “license certificate included”
- “provenance summary available”
- “technical quality checked”
