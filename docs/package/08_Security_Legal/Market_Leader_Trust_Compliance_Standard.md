# Market-Leader Trust and Compliance Standard

## Purpose

This document defines the trust and compliance posture required if EchoVault aims to become a market leader.

It does not replace legal counsel.

## Core promise

Allowed:

> EchoVault provides curated AI/hybrid music with documented provenance, versioned licenses, technical quality screening, human review and support workflows.

Not allowed without legal basis:

> EchoVault guarantees that every track is infringement-free.

## Required trust artifacts

Every approved/purchased track must be able to produce:

- Track ID.
- File checksum.
- Artist/creator record.
- PoE snapshot.
- EchoScore analysis version.
- Review decision and policy version.
- License terms version.
- Purchase record.
- Certificate ID.
- Delivered file list.
- Audit log for critical state changes.
- Dispute/suspension history if applicable.

## Legal/compliance DoD

Before public paid launch:
- Artist warranty reviewed by counsel.
- Buyer license reviewed by counsel.
- Dispute policy reviewed by counsel.
- Refund policy reviewed by counsel.
- Privacy policy reviewed by counsel.
- AI/provenance/copyright copy reviewed by counsel.
- Terms versioning implemented.
- Historical license immutability implemented.
- Data retention policy defined.
- Private PoE field visibility defined.

Before enterprise launch:
- Security posture documented.
- Vendor/subprocessor list created.
- Audit export exists.
- DPA template considered where relevant.
- SSO/team permission model implemented if required.
- Incident response and breach process documented.
- Procurement/security FAQ prepared.

## Public transparency standard

EchoVault should publish:
- What EchoScore measures.
- What EchoScore does not measure.
- What PoE means.
- What PoE does not prove.
- How review works at a high level.
- What happens if a track is disputed.
- How certificates can be verified.
- How terms versions work.

## Claims and disputes

A mature claims workflow must:
- Accept claims from buyer, artist, platform or third party.
- Link claim to track/license/certificate where possible.
- Suspend future sales when risk threshold is met.
- Preserve old license records.
- Create evidence pack.
- Track resolution outcome.
- Feed learnings back into policy and review.

## AI governance notes

If EchoVault uses third-party AI tools, models or automated decisioning:
- Document what systems are used.
- Avoid fully automated legal/rights decisions.
- Maintain human oversight for approval and dispute resolution.
- Keep technical documentation for AI-assisted scoring.
- Maintain copyright/provenance policy.
- Do not expose private user/artist data to model providers without proper terms and consent.

## Market context

AI music rights remain legally and commercially fluid. Competitors may obtain licensed training/content agreements or commercial-use models. EchoVault's trust posture must therefore be based on auditable process, rights documentation and buyer workflow value, not vague "AI-safe" marketing.
