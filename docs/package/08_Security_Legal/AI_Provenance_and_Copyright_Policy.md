# AI Provenance and Copyright Policy

## Purpose
Define how EchoVault handles AI/hybrid music provenance, rights warranties and copyright-risk controls.

## Position
EchoVault can document and reduce uncertainty. It cannot guarantee that no third party will ever assert a claim.

## Artist requirements
Artists must declare:
- creation mode,
- AI tools used,
- manual production/DAW work,
- sample usage,
- voice/likeness usage,
- whether named-artist imitation was used,
- rights warranty acceptance.

Artists must not submit:
- tracks containing uncleared samples,
- tracks intentionally imitating a named artist or voice without rights,
- tracks generated from prompts designed to clone protected works,
- tracks they cannot license commercially,
- tracks already subject to conflicting exclusive agreements.

## Platform requirements
EchoVault must require PoE before review, run technical QA before approval, use human review, maintain audit logs, version license terms, allow track suspension, maintain dispute records, and avoid “risk-free” marketing.

## Buyer-facing disclosure
Buyer-facing pages should say:
- AI/hybrid nature is documented.
- License certificate documents platform license grant and purchase.
- EchoScore is technical QA only.
- PoE is provenance documentation, not absolute legal proof.

## Legal review required
Before public launch, counsel must review artist terms/warranty, buyer license agreement, acceptable use, refund policy, takedown/dispute policy, privacy policy and content moderation policy.
