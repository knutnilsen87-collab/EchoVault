# Compliance Requirements

## Applicable regimes
- GDPR / equivalent: applies if serving EU/EEA users or storing personal data.
- Payment compliance: card data handled by Stripe; do not store card data.
- Copyright/licensing: central legal risk; external counsel required.
- EU AI Act watch item: transparency and AI-generated content/provenance obligations may affect labeling/disclosure. GPAI rules may affect upstream providers more directly, but EchoVault should maintain AI-use disclosure and provenance records.
- Consumer/digital goods rules: refund/disclosure rules depend on jurisdiction.
- Tax/VAT: digital goods/license sales may require VAT handling, likely through Stripe Tax or accounting review.

## Contractual requirements
- Artist warranty.
- Buyer license terms.
- Terms of service.
- Privacy policy.
- Refund/digital delivery policy.
- Dispute/takedown policy.
- Data processing terms if needed.

## Compliance controls
- Clickwrap terms acceptance before checkout.
- License terms version stored at purchase.
- Audit log for admin/legal-sensitive actions.
- Public disclosure of AI/hybrid nature.
- Data minimization for PoE.
- Right-to-delete process, except legal/accounting retention constraints.
