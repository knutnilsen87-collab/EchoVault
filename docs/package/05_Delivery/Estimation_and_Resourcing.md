# Estimation and Resourcing

## Team roles
- Product: founder/product lead
- Design: 0.25–0.5 FTE product designer
- Frontend: 1 full-stack engineer
- Backend: 1 full-stack/backend engineer
- Audio/Python: 0.25–0.5 FTE specialist or backend engineer
- QA: part-time QA or engineer-owned tests
- DevOps: engineer-owned with managed services
- Legal: external counsel
- Operations: founder or part-time reviewer for beta

## Estimated effort
- MVP: 6–10 weeks with 2 strong engineers and tight scope.
- Beta: 2–4 additional weeks for hardening, onboarding content, support workflows and better admin tools.
- Production hardening: 4–8 additional weeks depending on legal review, claim handling, payout automation and monitoring.

## Major effort risks
- Stripe webhook edge cases
- File upload/transcoding complexity
- Review queue UX
- Certificate generation
- Legal copy iteration
- Dispute support workflow
- Audio analysis threshold tuning
