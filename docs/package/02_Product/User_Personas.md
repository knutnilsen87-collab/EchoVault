# User Personas

## Persona 1 — Creator buyer
- Name: Nina the monetized creator
- Role: YouTube/Twitch creator
- Context: Publishes weekly videos and needs reliable background music.
- Pain points: copyright claims/demonetization, generic stock libraries, unclear AI rights.
- Goals: find music quickly, pay once, keep proof, avoid claim surprises.
- Buying/use trigger: new video deadline.

## Persona 2 — Small agency buyer
- Name: Martin the agency producer
- Role: Produces ads/social content for SMB clients
- Context: Needs many tracks across campaigns with clear client-use permission.
- Pain points: client compliance questions, subscription ambiguity, uneven AI quality.
- Goals: clear invoice/certificate, commercial permission, fast search/download.
- Buying/use trigger: campaign deadline and client asks “are we allowed to use this?”

## Persona 3 — Indie game developer
- Name: Sara the indie dev
- Role: Small studio/game developer
- Context: Needs affordable mood/loop music before paying a composer.
- Pain points: budget constrained, future takedown fear, needs prototype soundtrack.
- Goals: fast soundtrack, license clarity, potential future custom/originals upgrade.
- Buying/use trigger: trailer/demo release.

## Persona 4 — Hybrid producer / artist
- Name: Leo the hybrid producer
- Role: AI-assisted producer who edits/mixes in DAW
- Context: Wants to monetize tracks beyond streaming royalties.
- Pain points: noisy AI marketplaces, hard to prove effort/quality, wants premium positioning.
- Goals: submit tracks for review, get paid per license, build reputation.
- Buying/use trigger: needs distribution channel for high-quality AI/hybrid catalog.
