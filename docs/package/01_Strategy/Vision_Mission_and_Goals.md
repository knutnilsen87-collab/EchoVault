# Vision, Mission and Goals

## Vision statement
Make AI/hybrid music commercially usable by giving buyers a trusted, documented and efficient licensing path.

## Mission statement
Help creators and commercial teams license high-quality AI/hybrid music with less friction, clearer rights, stronger provenance and reliable file delivery.

## Strategic thesis
AI music supply will become abundant. The scarce asset will be trust: quality control, rights clarity, provenance, workflow speed and claim support. EchoVault should therefore be built as licensing infrastructure and trust layer, not a generic music-streaming destination.

## MVP goal
Prove that buyers will pay for curated AI/hybrid tracks because EchoVault reduces perceived licensing and quality risk.

## 90-day goals
1. Launch a closed beta catalog of 30–100 approved tracks.
2. Complete 15–25 buyer discovery interviews.
3. Secure 20–50 paid license purchases or letters of intent.
4. Measure buyer conversion, support requests and review cost per track.
5. Complete legal review of artist warranty, buyer license and dispute policy.

## Success description
EchoVault is succeeding when buyers purchase without founder hand-holding, licenses are issued idempotently through Stripe webhook flow, review decisions are consistent, artists understand PoE requirements, legal/support edge cases are tracked, and disputed tracks can be suspended without breaking existing license history.
