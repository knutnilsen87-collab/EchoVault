# Executive Summary

## What is being built
EchoVault is a focused MVP for the broader EchoVerse vision: a curated B2B marketplace where buyers can license AI/hybrid music with clear provenance, technical QA, license certificate, payment record and controlled file delivery.

## Why now
AI music tools have made music generation faster and cheaper, but commercial use still carries buyer anxiety: copyright claims, unclear provenance, uneven quality, content-policy risk and reputational risk. This creates an opening for a trust-first licensing workflow.

## Target users
Primary:
- YouTube/Twitch creators monetizing content
- Small agencies producing ads/social assets
- Motion designers and video editors
- Indie game developers and small studios
- SMB marketing teams

Secondary:
- AI-native or hybrid producers who can document their process and meet quality standards

## MVP summary
Artist upload → PoE submission → EchoScore QA → admin approval → buyer preview → license selection → Stripe payment → immutable license ledger → PDF certificate → private signed WAV delivery.

## Why it wins
EchoVault should not win by having “more AI music.” It should win by reducing buyer uncertainty:
- curated catalog
- technical audio QA
- creator process documentation
- license terms versioning
- certificate at purchase
- audit logs
- dispute intake

## Verdict
**PROCEED WITH SCOPE REDUCTION**

Do not build full EchoVerse B2C streaming first. Build EchoVault B2B licensing and validate willingness to pay.

## Key risk
The product may become a nice interface over a weak legal promise. The trust layer must be reviewed by counsel and validated by real buyers. Do not claim the product is “risk-free.”
