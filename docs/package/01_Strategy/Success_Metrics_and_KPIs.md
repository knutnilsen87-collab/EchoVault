# Success Metrics and KPIs

## North Star Metric
**Paid licenses issued per approved track per month**

Why: It connects catalog quality, buyer demand, licensing trust and monetization efficiency.

## MVP validation metrics
| Metric | Target for closed beta | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| Buyer interviews completed | 15–25 | Validates pain and decision criteria |
| Paid licenses issued | 20–50 | Validates willingness to pay |
| Catalog approval rate | 20–50% | Signals quality gate strength |
| Review time per submitted track | < 15 minutes median | Tests operational scalability |
| Checkout-to-license success rate | > 98% | Confirms fulfillment reliability |
| Support tickets per 10 purchases | < 3 | Measures clarity and trust |
| Refund/dispute rate | < 5% | Early quality/legal-risk signal |
| Repeat buyer rate | > 20% in beta cohort | Early retention signal |
