# V2 Addendum — Total Product DoD and Market-Leader Target State

**Date:** 2026-06-04  
**Package:** EchoVault / EchoVerse CTO Developer Package v2  
**Purpose:** This addendum expands the original MVP-focused handoff into a full target-state product and market-leader operating model.

## What changed from v1

v1 defined a strong MVP execution package: catalog, upload, review, EchoScore, PoE, Stripe checkout, license ledger, PDF certificate and signed file delivery.

v2 adds the missing strategic and operational layer:

1. **Total Product Definition of Done** — what "done" means beyond individual tickets and beyond MVP.
2. **Definition of Dominance** — what must be true for EchoVault to become the category leader in trusted AI/hybrid music licensing.
3. **Full Product Surface Map** — buyer, artist, admin, trust, data, legal, marketplace, enterprise and API surfaces.
4. **Optimal Product Experience** — how the product should feel and function when mature.
5. **Target-State Architecture** — what the platform must eventually support without rebuilding the core.
6. **MVP-to-Market-Leader Roadmap** — how to sequence from first wedge to category platform without fantasy scaling.

## Evidence labels

- **Observed:** Comes from the user-provided EchoVerse blueprint or current public source notes.
- **Inferred:** Strategic conclusion based on the blueprint and market structure.
- **Speculative:** Must be validated through users, legal counsel, data or real marketplace behavior.

## Critical warning

This package still does **not** validate product-market fit. It defines what to build and how to judge readiness, but market leadership can only be proven through actual buyer behavior, repeat purchases, artist supply quality, legal resilience and distribution advantage.
