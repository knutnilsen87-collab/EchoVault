# External Source Notes

These sources were used only to ground market/legal risk notes. The product package remains a strategic/technical draft and requires legal review.

## Sources checked

1. Suno Help Center — Rights & Ownership
   - Relevant note: Suno states that songs made while subscribed to Pro/Premier are granted commercial use rights.
   - Use in this package: competitive substitute risk.

2. Mubert public product/licensing pages
   - Relevant note: Mubert positions itself as royalty-free AI music for content creators and provides license/certificate-style commercial plans.
   - Use in this package: direct/substitute competitor risk.

3. RIAA public lawsuit materials and Reuters reporting on Suno/Udio litigation
   - Relevant note: major labels sued Suno/Udio over alleged mass copyright infringement; later reporting indicates movement toward licensed AI music products/settlements.
   - Use in this package: legal-risk and market-direction signal.

4. European Commission AI Act / GPAI / transparency guidance pages
   - Relevant note: AI Act transparency/copyright-related rules and synthetic-content labeling obligations are relevant watch items.
   - Use in this package: compliance watch item.

## Legal note

Nothing in this package is legal advice. Counsel must review buyer license, artist warranty, terms of service, refund policy, takedown/dispute policy and privacy policy before public launch.
