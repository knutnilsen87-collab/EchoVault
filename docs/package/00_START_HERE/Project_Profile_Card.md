## One-sentence description
EchoVault is a curated B2B licensing marketplace for AI/hybrid music, combining technical quality screening, Proof of Effort provenance, Stripe checkout, private file delivery and license certificates.

## Core problem
Commercial buyers need fast, affordable music for videos, ads, games and creator content, but AI-generated music creates trust gaps around quality, rights, provenance, Content ID risk and reputational risk.

## Core user
Primary: YouTube/Twitch creators, small agencies, motion designers, indie game developers and SMB content teams who need music quickly and want a clearer license trail than ad-hoc AI generation.

Secondary: AI/hybrid music creators who want monetization and premium positioning without competing in low-quality AI spam markets.

## Core job to be done
“When I need music for commercial content, help me find a usable track, verify the usage rights, pay, and get the files/certificate fast without legal or copyright-claim anxiety.”

## MVP definition
A working vertical slice where an artist uploads a track and PoE form, EchoScore runs as technical QA, an admin approves/rejects, a buyer previews an approved track, purchases a license through Stripe, and receives a PDF certificate plus expiring high-res WAV download URL.
